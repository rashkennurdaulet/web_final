A Day Out in the Wild

City Zoo is home to more than 150 species across 50 hectares of open-air habitats. Walk from the African savannah to the polar pavilion in a single afternoon, meet keepers at the daily shows, and stop for lunch at our eco-cafes.

Small snacks and water bottles are welcome. For picnics, please use the family rest areas or visit one of our eco-cafes.
